Why Uploading Sensitive PDFs to Cloud Converters is Dangerous

Every time you drag and drop an employment agreement, bank statement, tax filing, or medical diagnostic report into traditional web converters, the raw binary file is transmitted over the internet to a remote datacenter. Even when services claim to "delete your file after 2 hours," your data is exposed to potential server breaches, cache retention bugs, and employee inspection.

🚨 Server-Side Breaches

Centralized cloud converter databases are prime targets for cyberattacks. Temporary storage buckets often experience misconfigurations that expose user documents publicly.

🏢 Corporate NDA & HIPAA Violations

Uploading client documents or proprietary code to unauthorized cloud services directly violates enterprise data protection policies and GDPR compliance mandates.

🔒 The WebAssembly Solution

AuraToolkit360 uses WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript engines (pdf-lib, pdf.js). Your browser performs the mathematical transformations locally on your hardware.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about switching from iLovePDF to AuraToolkit360.

❓ How can AuraToolkit360 convert PDFs without uploading them?
Modern web browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) have native access to WebAssembly and high-performance JavaScript engines. AuraToolkit360 downloads the conversion logic into your browser cache once. From that moment on, all page parsing, merging, text extraction, and compression happen directly on your device's processor.
❓ Is there really no file size limit? What if my PDF is 300MB?
Because we do not have server hosting or egress bandwidth bills for your files, we don't need to impose artificial caps like 15MB or 25MB. As long as your device has sufficient RAM to open the document, AuraToolkit360 can process it seamlessly.
❓ Will my formatting be preserved during PDF to Word conversion?
Yes. Our client-side parser scans the coordinate stream of the PDF, grouping text elements into semantic headings, paragraphs, and lists so that your exported DOCX file retains its original structure.
❓ How does AuraToolkit360 compare to Smallpdf?
Smallpdf limits free users to just 1 or 2 document operations per day and requires an expensive $9/month recurring plan. AuraToolkit360 is 100% free with unlimited conversions, zero daily lockouts, and complete in-browser privacy.
