Why Choose Client-Side In-Browser Compression?

Traditional PDF compressors (like Smallpdf or cloud portals) upload your full PDF binary to external web servers. When compressing bank statements, tax forms, or company balance sheets, this introduces severe data leak liabilities.

AuraToolkit360 solves this completely: All byte reduction occurs directly inside your browser using PDF-Lib stream serialization. Your documents are never uploaded to any remote server, preserving 100% data confidentiality.
