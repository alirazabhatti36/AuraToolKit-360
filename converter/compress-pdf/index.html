Why Choose Client-Side In-Browser Compression?

Traditional PDF compressors (like Smallpdf or cloud portals) upload your full PDF binary to external web servers. When compressing bank statements, tax forms, or company balance sheets, this introduces severe data leak liabilities.

AuraToolkit360 solves this completely: All byte reduction occurs directly inside your browser using PDF-Lib stream serialization. Your documents are never uploaded to any remote server, preserving 100% data confidentiality.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ Can I compress my PDF to under 100KB or 200KB for application portals?
Yes! Select the "Maximum Compression" preset. AuraToolkit360 aggressively strips redundant dictionary streams and metadata to produce compact file sizes acceptable on job and exam portals.
❓ Does compression make the text blurry?
No! Unlike raster image converters that downgrade resolution, our engine optimizes the underlying PDF object stream structure, keeping vector typography crisp and clean at 100% zoom.
❓ Is there any file size limit for free users?
No. You can compress large PDF files (50MB–100MB+) with zero subscription fees, credit card requirements, or hourly lockouts.
