How to Capture and Prepare a Perfect Digital Specimen Signature

Digital application validation systems reject up to 14% of submitted signatures due to paper shadows, colored backgrounds, or incorrect aspect ratios. Follow these 4 steps to guarantee approval:

  • Use Pure White Unlined Paper: Always sign on clean white photocopy paper. Never sign on ruled or notebook lines, which can be interpreted as stray pixels.
  • Use Dark Blue or Black Ink: Gel pens or fine rollerballs provide deep, consistent ink density. Avoid light ballpoint pens or pencils that appear washed out when scanned.
  • Ensure Shadow-Free Lighting: Hold your phone directly parallel over the signature in bright daylight or under a desk lamp to prevent casting your hand's shadow across the writing.
  • Crop Tightly: Before uploading, crop the image tightly around the ink strokes to maximize signature visibility within the final 140x60 container.
