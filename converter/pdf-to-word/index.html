Why Client-Side PDF to Word Conversion Protects Your Privacy

Most commercial file conversion services (like Smallpdf or legacy cloud portals) require transmitting your sensitive contracts, tax records, employee resumes, and legal filings to remote third-party cloud datacenters. Even if deleted later, your data is exposed to server misconfigurations and unauthorized interceptors.

AuraToolkit360 eliminates this risk: By running 100% of the conversion logic inside your local browser via WebAssembly and JavaScript, your document never leaves your machine. This guarantees complete compliance with GDPR, HIPAA, and corporate confidentiality policies.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ Will my converted Word document retain editable text?
Yes! Unlike primitive screenshot converters, AuraToolkit360 analyzes font metrics and vertical text offsets to produce genuine editable text paragraphs and multi-page structures in Microsoft Word format.

No. Because all computation runs on your local device hardware, there are no artificial hourly caps, daily limits, or paywalls.
❓ Can I convert scanned PDF documents?
