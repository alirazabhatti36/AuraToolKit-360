Essential Percentage Math Formulas

A percentage is a number or ratio expressed as a fraction of 100. Understanding fundamental formulas simplifies daily business accounting, grade assessments, and retail budget planning.

  • Percentage of Value: P = (X × Y) / 100
  • Ratio to Percentage: Percentage = (X / Y) × 100
  • Percentage Change: ((New Value - Old Value) / Old Value) × 100
  • Discount Price: Discounted Price = Original Price - (Original Price × Discount% / 100)
