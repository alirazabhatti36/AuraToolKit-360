Rules of Zakat and Nisab Threshold Guidelines

Zakat is one of the Five Pillars of Islam, obligatory on every sane adult Muslim who possesses wealth equal to or exceeding the Nisab threshold continuously for one lunar year (Hawl).

  • Silver Nisab: Approximately 612.36 grams (52.5 Tolas) of silver. Most scholars recommend using the silver threshold to maximize benefit to the needy.
  • Gold Nisab: Approximately 87.48 grams (7.5 Tolas) of gold.
  • Rate: Exactly 2.5% (one fortieth) of net qualifying wealth after deducting immediate liabilities.
