Video Compression & Media Optimization Guide for Web & Messaging

Sharing large video recordings across Discord, email, WhatsApp, or LMS portals is often blocked by strict file size ceilings (such as 10MB or 25MB). AuraToolkit360 guides efficient media bitrate optimization with complete client privacy.

Factors Determining Video File Size

  • Resolution Scaling: Downscaling a 4K video (3840x2160) to Full HD (1920x1080) reduces pixel data by 75% without compromising clarity on phone screens.
  • Bitrate Control: Lowering video bitrate from 12 Mbps to 2.5 Mbps achieves massive compression while maintaining smooth playback.
  • Modern Codecs (H.264 & VP9): Deliver superior visual fidelity per kilobyte compared to legacy MPEG formats.
