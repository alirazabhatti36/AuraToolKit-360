Supported Measurement Units & Standard Scales

AuraToolkit360 supports standard International System of Units (SI), Imperial measurements, as well as regionally recognized land and weight units (such as Marla, Kanal, and Tola).

Key Regional Measurement Standards

  • Marla (Lahore/Punjab Standard): 1 Marla is officially recognized as 225 square feet (approx 25 square yards). The older British revenue standard is 272.25 square feet.
  • Kanal: 1 Kanal equals 20 Marlas = 4,500 square feet (or 5,445 sq ft in revenue records).
  • Tola (Precious Metals): Traditional South Asian unit used for gold and silver, officially standardized at 11.664 grams (1 Tola = 12 Mashas).
  • Digital Data (Binary 1024): 1 Kilobyte (KB) = 1,024 Bytes, 1 Megabyte (MB) = 1,024 KB, 1 Gigabyte (GB) = 1,024 MB.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit manually?

Multiply degrees Celsius by 9/5 (or 1.8) and then add 32: °F = (°C × 1.8) + 32.

❓ How many pounds are in 1 kilogram?

1 kilogram is equal to approximately 2.20462 pounds (lbs).
