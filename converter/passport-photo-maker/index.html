Essential Rules for Passport Photo Approval

  • No Eyeglasses: Eyeglasses are prohibited by US State Department and European consular offices to prevent reflection over eyes.
  • Neutral Expression: Look directly into the camera lens with both eyes open and mouth closed. Avoid exaggerated smiling or frowning.
  • Head Coverage: Head coverings worn for religious purposes are permitted, provided the full oval of the face from forehead hairline to chin is visible.
  • Contrasting Clothing: Wear dark or colored everyday attire that contrasts clearly with the white background. Avoid white shirts.
