Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ How are Business Days calculated in the date difference tool?

The date difference tool counts Monday through Friday and automatically excludes Saturdays and Sundays between the selected dates.

❓ How many seconds are in a 24-hour day?

There are exactly 86,400 seconds in a day (24 hours × 60 minutes × 60 seconds).
