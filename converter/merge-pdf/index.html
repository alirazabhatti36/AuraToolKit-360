Why Client-Side PDF Merging is the Safest Option

Commercial services often enforce strict 15MB or 20-page limits on free accounts, demanding $10–$20 monthly subscriptions to combine larger reports. More critically, uploading company tax filings, legal agreements, or health records to cloud servers exposes your data to server-side retention and breaches.

AuraToolkit360 solves both issues: Merging is performed locally on your device's CPU. Your files never touch remote cloud servers, and there are zero artificial size caps or paywalls.

Yes. AuraToolkit360 uses PDF-Lib to copy native page streams without rasterizing them, ensuring text remains sharp and selectable.
