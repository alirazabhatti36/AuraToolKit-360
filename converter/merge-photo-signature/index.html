Combine Photo and Signature Online for Official Application Forms

Competitive exams and public service commission forms frequently require a single combined JPG image featuring the candidate's passport photo on top and their digital signature underneath. AuraToolkit360 aligns both assets into a single certified composite file.

Official Guidelines for Combined Documents

  • Vertical Alignment: The passport photograph occupies the top 70% of the composite canvas, while the signature occupies the bottom 30%.
  • Consistent White Background: Both assets should feature matching clean white backgrounds to ensure seamless merging.
  • Zero Server Upload Privacy: Processing occurs in local device memory, guaranteeing complete security for your personal credentials.
