Clean Spreadsheet Sharing Without Formula Alteration

Distributing raw Excel spreadsheets can accidentally expose complex proprietary formulas, hidden financial rows, or allow unauthorized alterations. Converting spreadsheets to PDF creates an immutable, professional snapshot of your data tables that anyone can view without spreadsheet software.
