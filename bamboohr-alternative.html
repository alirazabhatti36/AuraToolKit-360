Frequently Asked Questions

Why is AuraToolKit 360 a better choice than BambooHR?
BambooHR is tailored for large corporate budgets with high per-seat billing and charges extra for payroll and hardware attendance sync. AuraToolKit 360 is built for agility: flat transparent pricing, native Hikvision biometric attendance, integrated ATS, and zero hidden add-on costs.
Can we migrate existing employee records?
Yes. AuraToolKit 360 supports standard CSV batch imports for employee rosters, previous attendance logs, and payroll records.
How does the Hikvision biometric bridge work?
Our lightweight bridge script runs on any local office computer connected to the same LAN as your Hikvision machine. It listens for check-in punches and transmits encrypted logs directly to your cloud dashboard in real time.
