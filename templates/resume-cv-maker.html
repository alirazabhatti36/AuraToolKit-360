Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ How does client-side resume building protect my personal info?
Unlike typical resume builders that store your phone number, email, and work history in remote databases, AuraToolkit360 runs entirely in your browser. Your data never touches a server.
❓ Are the resume templates ATS (Applicant Tracking System) friendly?
Yes! All templates use clean typography, clear section headings, and standard text structures easily parsed by ATS screening software.
❓ Can I save my resume draft and edit it later?
Yes! Use the "Save JSON Draft" button to download your data file, and click "Load JSON Draft" anytime to restore your resume for quick edits.
