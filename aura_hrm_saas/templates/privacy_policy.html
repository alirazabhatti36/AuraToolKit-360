Last updated: August 12, 2026

At AuraToolKit 360 (accessible from https://auratoolkit360.com), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and B2B SaaS tenants. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by AuraToolKit 360 and how we use it, in full compliance with Google AdSense Policies, GDPR, and international data protection standards.

When you register for a B2B company account or submit a inquiry form, we may collect the following personal and organizational information:

  • Company name, domain, official email address, and phone number.

AuraToolKit 360 uses third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on AuraToolKit 360.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Multi-Tenant Data Isolation & Security

We implement strict database security protocols and multi-tenant isolation algorithms to guarantee that company payroll, CNIC records, and salary details remain 100% confidential and inaccessible to other organizations.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, AuraToolKit 360 uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, session states, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. For detailed information, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by email at privacy@auratoolkit360.com.
