About AuraToolKit 360

Enterprise B2B Multi-Tenant HR & Payroll Infrastructure

Our Mission

AuraToolKit 360 was founded with a singular mission: to simplify workforce operations for growing software houses, agencies, factories, and global enterprise organizations. We provide a unified, multi-tenant SaaS platform that integrates recruitment candidate ATS filtering, instant Hikvision biometric device hardware synchronization, localized country payroll, and asset inventory management.

Why Businesses Choose AuraToolKit 360

  • Strict Tenant Isolation: Every client company operates in a 100% confidential, isolated database container.
  • Biometric Connectivity: Real-time automated syncing with physical Hikvision office terminals.
  • Global Flexibility: Multi-currency pricing and multi-language support across Pakistan, USA, Gulf, Europe, and Asia.
