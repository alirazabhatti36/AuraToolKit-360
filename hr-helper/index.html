Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

            

                
❓ Is this tool free for recruiters and small HR teams?

                
Yes! AuraToolkit360 HR Bulk Helper is 100% free with no candidate processing limits or fees.

            

            

                
❓ How are candidate resumes ranked?

                
Resumes are scored based on skill keyword matching, title relevance, and formatting quality against your specified job description.

            

            

                
❓ Are candidate resumes stored on your servers?

                
No. All file extraction and keyword matching runs 100% locally in your web browser via WebAssembly and JavaScript. Zero resumes are uploaded or saved.
