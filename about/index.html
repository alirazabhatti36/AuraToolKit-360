About AuraToolKit 360

Empowering individuals, students, and professionals with privacy-first client-side web utilities.

Welcome to AuraToolKit 360, a comprehensive digital workspace engineered to handle everyday document conversions, image processing, ATS resume optimization, and recruitment screening without compromising your privacy or charging subscription fees.

Our Mission & Philosophy

Most commercial utility websites force users into restrictive paywalls, require intrusive account creation, or upload sensitive documents to remote cloud servers where retention policies remain opaque. AuraToolKit 360 was built on a different philosophy: Zero-Knowledge, Client-Side First.

We believe fundamental digital utilities—such as merging confidential legal contracts, converting sensitive financial spreadsheets, or crafting career resumes—should execute entirely on your device whenever technologically possible. By executing heavy computation in modern browser runtimes, your private files never travel over the network to third-party databases.

Our Core Engineering Architecture

Behind the clean dark glassmorphism interface of AuraToolKit 360 is a robust client-side tech stack designed for speed, resilience, and privacy:

  • WebAssembly & Web Workers: Intensive PDF manipulation (merging, splitting, encryption, compression) and optical character recognition (OCR) leverage isolated background threads so your browser UI stays smooth and responsive.
  • HTML5 Canvas & MediaRecorder API: Image processing (cropping, signature resizing, passport photo generation) and video compression execute directly against hardware-accelerated browser APIs without server transmission.
  • Dynamic Local Storage: Resume drafts and screening sessions are saved securely in your browser's sandboxed local storage, ensuring your job applications remain strictly private to you.
  • Zero File Retention: Because operations are handled client-side in RAM, files are permanently cleared from memory the moment you close or refresh your browser tab.
