Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ Do recruiters actually read cover letters in 2026?
Yes! While ATS scans resumes for keyword matching, hiring managers read cover letters to assess communication skills, cultural fit, and passion for the specific role. A tailored cover letter increases interview call-backs by up to 40%.
❓ Does this cover letter match my resume formatting?
Absolutely! You can choose the exact same accent color, font family (Inter, Roboto, Merriweather, Poppins), and paper color tint as your AuraToolkit360 resume for a unified application portfolio.
❓ Can I export to editable Microsoft Word (DOCX)?
Yes! 1-Click exports generate both vector-quality print PDFs and cleanly formatted Microsoft Word DOCX files with zero watermarks or subscriptions.
