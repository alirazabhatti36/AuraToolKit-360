Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ Are my uploaded files stored on any server?
No! AuraToolkit360 operates with 100% client-side technology. Your files are processed entirely within your web browser's local memory and are never uploaded or stored anywhere.
❓ Does PDF to Word conversion preserve text paragraphs?
Yes! Our PDF to Word converter analyzes exact line positioning and paragraph coordinates to preserve multi-line text structures, bullet points, and headings in the generated DOCX file.
❓ Can I extract text from scanned images using OCR?
Yes, our built-in Optical Character Recognition (OCR) engine recognizes text inside PNG, JPG, and WEBP images directly in your browser and exports to TXT, Word DOCX, or Excel XLSX formats.
